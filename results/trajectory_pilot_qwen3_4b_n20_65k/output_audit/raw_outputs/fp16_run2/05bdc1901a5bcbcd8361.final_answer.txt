

To solve the system of inequalities:

$$
0 \ge 54p - 144 \quad \text{and} \quad 0 > 12 - 20p
$$

we will solve each inequality separately and then find their intersection.

---

### **Step 1: Solve the first inequality**

$$
0 \ge 54p - 144
$$

Rewriting:

$$
54p - 144 \le 0
$$

Add 144 to both sides:

$$
54p \le 144
$$

Divide both sides by 54:

$$
p \le \frac{144}{54}
$$

Simplify the fraction:

$$
\frac{144}{54} = \frac{8}{3}
$$

So, the first inequality gives:

$$
p \le \frac{8}{3}
$$

---

### **Step 2: Solve the second inequality**

$$
0 > 12 - 20p
$$

Rewriting:

$$
12 - 20p < 0
$$

Subtract 12 from both sides:

$$
-20p < -12
$$

Divide both sides by -20 (and reverse the inequality):

$$
p > \frac{12}{20}
$$

Simplify the fraction:

$$
\frac{12}{20} = \frac{3}{5}
$$

So, the second inequality gives:

$$
p > \frac{3}{5}
$$

---

### **Step 3: Combine the two inequalities**

We now have:

$$
\frac{3}{5} < p \le \frac{8}{3}
$$

This is the set of all real numbers $ p $ that are **greater than** $ \frac{3}{5} $ and **less than or equal to** $ \frac{8}{3} $.

---

### **Final Answer in Interval Notation**

$$
\boxed{\left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{8}{3} \right]}
$$
