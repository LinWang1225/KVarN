

To solve the equation:

$$
10^x - 10 = 9990
$$

we begin by isolating the exponential term $10^x$. This is done by adding 10 to both sides of the equation:

$$
10^x = 9990 + 10
$$

$$
10^x = 10000
$$

Now, we need to solve for $x$ in the equation:

$$
10^x = 10000
$$

We recognize that 10000 is a power of 10. Specifically:

$$
10^4 = 10000
$$

Therefore, since the bases are the same, the exponents must be equal:

$$
x = 4
$$

---

To confirm, we substitute $x = 4$ back into the original equation:

$$
10^4 - 10 = 10000 - 10 = 9990
$$

This matches the right-hand side of the equation, verifying that the solution is correct.

---

**Final Answer:**

$$
\boxed{4}
$$
