

To determine the difference between the **positive square root of 64** and the **cube root of 64**, we begin by identifying each root individually.

---

### Step 1: Find the Positive Square Root of 64

The **square root** of a number is a value that, when multiplied by itself, gives the original number. The **positive square root** of 64 is:

$$
\sqrt{64} = 8
$$

This is because:

$$
8 \times 8 = 64
$$

---

### Step 2: Find the Cube Root of 64

The **cube root** of a number is a value that, when multiplied by itself three times, gives the original number. The **cube root** of 64 is:

$$
\sqrt[3]{64} = 4
$$

This is because:

$$
4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64
$$

---

### Step 3: Calculate the Difference

The question asks for the **difference between the positive square root of 64 and the cube root of 64**. This is interpreted as:

$$
\text{Positive square root} - \text{Cube root} = 8 - 4 = 4
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{4}
$$
