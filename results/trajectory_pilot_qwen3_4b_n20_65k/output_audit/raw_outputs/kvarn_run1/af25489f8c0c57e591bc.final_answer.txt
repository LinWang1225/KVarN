

To determine the **total surface area** of a cube with a **volume of 1 cubic foot**, we need to follow a clear sequence of steps involving unit conversion and the geometric formulas for a cube.

---

### Step 1: Understand the Volume of a Cube

The volume $ V $ of a cube is given by the formula:

$$
V = s^3
$$

where $ s $ is the length of one side of the cube in the same unit as the volume.

Given that the volume is **1 cubic foot**, we can solve for the side length $ s $:

$$
s = \sqrt[3]{1} = 1 \text{ foot}
$$

---

### Step 2: Convert the Side Length to Inches

Since the final answer must be in **square inches**, we need to convert the side length from **feet to inches**.

$$
1 \text{ foot} = 12 \text{ inches}
$$

So, the side length in inches is:

$$
s = 12 \text{ inches}
$$

---

### Step 3: Use the Surface Area Formula

The **surface area** $ A $ of a cube is given by:

$$
A = 6s^2
$$

Substituting $ s = 12 $ inches:

$$
A = 6 \times (12)^2 = 6 \times 144 = 864 \text{ square inches}
$$

---

### Step 4: Cross-Verify with an Alternative Method

Alternatively, we can convert the **volume from cubic feet to cubic inches** first:

$$
1 \text{ cubic foot} = 12 \times 12 \times 12 = 1728 \text{ cubic inches}
$$

Now, the side length in inches is:

$$
s = \sqrt[3]{1728} = 12 \text{ inches}
$$

Then, using the same surface area formula:

$$
A = 6 \times (12)^2 = 6 \times 144 = 864 \text{ square inches}
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{864}
$$

The total surface area of the cube is **864 square inches**.
