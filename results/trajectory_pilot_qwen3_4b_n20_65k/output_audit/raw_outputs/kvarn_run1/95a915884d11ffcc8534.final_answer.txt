

To solve the problem, we begin by defining the two-digit number in terms of its digits. Let the tens digit be $ a $ and the units digit be $ b $. Then, the original number is:

$$
10a + b
$$

When the digits are reversed, the number becomes:

$$
10b + a
$$

---

### Step 1: Use the given conditions

We are told two things:

1. The **sum of the digits** is 13:
   $$
   a + b = 13
   $$

2. The **difference between the original number and the reversed number** is 27:
   $$
   (10a + b) - (10b + a) = 27
   $$

Simplify the second equation:

$$
10a + b - 10b - a = 27 \\
9a - 9b = 27 \\
9(a - b) = 27 \\
a - b = 3
$$

---

### Step 2: Solve the system of equations

We now have the system:

$$
\begin{cases}
a + b = 13 \\
a - b = 3
\end{cases}
$$

Add the two equations:

$$
(a + b) + (a - b) = 13 + 3 \\
2a = 16 \\
a = 8
$$

Substitute $ a = 8 $ into $ a + b = 13 $:

$$
8 + b = 13 \\
b = 5
$$

---

### Step 3: Find the original and reversed numbers

- Original number: $ 10a + b = 10 \cdot 8 + 5 = 85 $
- Reversed number: $ 10b + a = 10 \cdot 5 + 8 = 58 $

---

### Step 4: Compute the sum of the original and reversed numbers

$$
85 + 58 = 143
$$

Alternatively, we can use a shortcut:

$$
(10a + b) + (10b + a) = 11a + 11b = 11(a + b) = 11 \cdot 13 = 143
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{143}
$$
