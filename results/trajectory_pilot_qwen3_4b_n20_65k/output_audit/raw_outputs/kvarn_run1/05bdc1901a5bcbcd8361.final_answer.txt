

To solve the system of inequalities:

$$
0 \ge 54p - 144 \quad \text{and} \quad 0 > 12 - 20p
$$

we will solve each inequality separately and then find their intersection.

---

### Step 1: Solve the first inequality

$$
0 \ge 54p - 144
$$

Rewriting it:

$$
54p - 144 \le 0
$$

Add 144 to both sides:

$$
54p \le 144
$$

Divide both sides by 54:

$$
p \le \frac{144}{54}
$$

Simplify the fraction:

$$
\frac{144}{54} = \frac{8}{3}
$$

So, the first inequality gives:

$$
p \le \frac{8}{3}
$$

---

### Step 2: Solve the second inequality

$$
0 > 12 - 20p
$$

Rewriting it:

$$
12 - 20p < 0
$$

Subtract 12 from both sides:

$$
-20p < -12
$$

Divide both sides by -20 (and reverse the inequality sign):

$$
p > \frac{12}{20}
$$

Simplify the fraction:

$$
\frac{12}{20} = \frac{3}{5}
$$

So, the second inequality gives:

$$
p > \frac{3}{5}
$$

---

### Step 3: Combine the two inequalities

We now have:

$$
\frac{3}{5} < p \le \frac{8}{3}
$$

This is the set of all values of $ p $ that satisfy **both** inequalities.

---

### Step 4: Express in interval notation

The inequality $ \frac{3}{5} < p \le \frac{8}{3} $ corresponds to the interval:

$$
\left( \frac{3}{5}, \frac{8}{3} \right]
$$

This is because:

- $ \frac{3}{5} $ is **not included** (open parenthesis),
- $ \frac{8}{3} $ is **included** (closed parenthesis).

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{\left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{8}{3} \right]}
$$
