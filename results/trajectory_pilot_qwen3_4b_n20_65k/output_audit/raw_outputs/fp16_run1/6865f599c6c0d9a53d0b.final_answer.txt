

To simplify the expression:

$$
\frac{\sqrt{2.5^2 - 0.7^2}}{2.7 - 2.5}
$$

---

### Step 1: Simplify the Numerator

We begin by computing the expression inside the square root:

$$
2.5^2 = 6.25, \quad 0.7^2 = 0.49
$$

$$
2.5^2 - 0.7^2 = 6.25 - 0.49 = 5.76
$$

Now, take the square root:

$$
\sqrt{5.76} = 2.4
$$

So the numerator simplifies to **2.4**.

---

### Step 2: Simplify the Denominator

$$
2.7 - 2.5 = 0.2
$$

So the denominator is **0.2**.

---

### Step 3: Divide the Simplified Numerator by the Denominator

$$
\frac{2.4}{0.2}
$$

To simplify this, we can convert the decimals to fractions:

$$
2.4 = \frac{24}{10}, \quad 0.2 = \frac{2}{10}
$$

$$
\frac{24}{10} \div \frac{2}{10} = \frac{24}{10} \times \frac{10}{2} = \frac{24 \times 10}{10 \times 2} = \frac{24}{2} = 12
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{12}
$$
