$$
\frac{C}{x-3} + \frac{D}{x+8} = \frac{4x - 23}{x^2 + 5x - 24}
$$

we begin by factoring the denominator on the right-hand side:

$$
x^2 + 5x - 24 = (x + 8)(x - 3)
$$

This allows us to rewrite the equation as:

$$
\frac{C}{x - 3} + \frac{D}{x + 8} = \frac{4x - 23}{(x + 8)(x - 3)}
$$

---

### Step 1: Combine the Left-Hand Side

To combine the two fractions on the left, we find a common denominator:

$$
\frac{C}{x - 3} + \frac{D}{x + 8} = \frac{C(x + 8) + D(x - 3)}{(x + 8)(x - 3)}
$$

So the equation becomes:

$$
\frac{C(x + 8) + D(x - 3)}{(x + 8)(x - 3)} = \frac{4x - 23}{(x + 8)(x - 3)}
$$

Since the denominators are the same, we equate the numerators:

$$
C(x + 8) + D(x - 3) = 4x - 23
$$

---

### Step 2: Expand and Match Coefficients

Expanding the left-hand side:

$$
C(x + 8) + D(x - 3) = Cx + 8C + Dx - 3D = (C + D)x + (8C - 3D)
$$

So we have:

$$
(C + D)x + (8C - 3D) = 4x - 23
$$

Now, equating coefficients of like terms:

- Coefficient of $ x $: $ C + D = 4 $
- Constant term: $ 8C - 3D = -23 $

D = 4 - C
