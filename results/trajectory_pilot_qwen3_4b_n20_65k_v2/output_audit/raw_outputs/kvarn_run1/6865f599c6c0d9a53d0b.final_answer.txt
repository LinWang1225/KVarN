

To simplify the expression:

$$
\frac{\sqrt{2.5^2 - 0.7^2}}{2.7 - 2.5}
$$

we proceed step by step, using algebraic identities and arithmetic.

---

### Step 1: Simplify the Numerator

The numerator is:

$$
\sqrt{2.5^2 - 0.7^2}
$$

We can apply the **difference of squares** identity:

$$
a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)
$$

Let $ a = 2.5 $ and $ b = 0.7 $. Then:

$$
2.5^2 - 0.7^2 = (2.5 - 0.7)(2.5 + 0.7) = (1.8)(3.2)
$$

Now compute the product:

$$
1.8 \times 3.2 = 5.76
$$

So the numerator becomes:

$$
\sqrt{5.76}
$$

We know that:

$$
\sqrt{5.76} = 2.4
$$

---

### Step 2: Simplify the Denominator

The denominator is:

$$
2.7 - 2.5 = 0.2
$$

---

### Step 3: Divide the Simplified Parts

Now we divide the simplified numerator by the simplified denominator:

$$
\frac{2.4}{0.2}
$$

Dividing by 0.2 is the same as multiplying by 5:

$$
2.4 \times 5 = 12
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{12}
$$
