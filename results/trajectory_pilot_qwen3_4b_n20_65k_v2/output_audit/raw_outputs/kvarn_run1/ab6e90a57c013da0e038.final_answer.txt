

To solve the equation:

$$
10^x - 10 = 9990
$$

we begin by isolating the exponential term. This is a standard approach when dealing with equations involving exponents.

---

### Step 1: Isolate the Exponential Term

Add 10 to both sides of the equation:

$$
10^x - 10 + 10 = 9990 + 10
$$

$$
10^x = 10000
$$

---

### Step 2: Express 10000 as a Power of 10

We recognize that:

$$
10^4 = 10000
$$

So, we can equate the two expressions:

$$
10^x = 10^4
$$

Since the bases are the same, the exponents must be equal:

$$
x = 4
$$

---

### Step 3: Verify the Solution

Substitute $ x = 4 $ back into the original equation:

$$
10^4 - 10 = 10000 - 10 = 9990
$$

This matches the right-hand side of the equation, confirming that the solution is correct.

---

### Step 4: Alternative Verification Using Logarithms

We can also solve this using logarithms. Taking the base-10 logarithm of both sides:

$$
\log_{10}(10^x) = \log_{10}(10000)
$$

$$
x = \log_{10}(10000)
$$

Since $ 10000 = 10^4 $, we have:

$$
x = \log_{10}(10^4) = 4
$$

This again confirms that $ x = 4 $.

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{4}
$$
