

To simplify the expression:

$$
(u + 4)(u - 1) - (u - 3)(u + 6)
$$

we begin by expanding each of the two binomial products using the distributive property (FOIL method).

---

### Step 1: Expand the first product

$$
(u + 4)(u - 1) = u \cdot u + u \cdot (-1) + 4 \cdot u + 4 \cdot (-1) = u^2 - u + 4u - 4 = u^2 + 3u - 4
$$

---

### Step 2: Expand the second product

$$
(u - 3)(u + 6) = u \cdot u + u \cdot 6 + (-3) \cdot u + (-3) \cdot 6 = u^2 + 6u - 3u - 18 = u^2 + 3u - 18
$$

---

### Step 3: Subtract the second expanded expression from the first

$$
(u^2 + 3u - 4) - (u^2 + 3u - 18)
$$

Distribute the negative sign across the second expression:

$$
u^2 + 3u - 4 - u^2 - 3u + 18
$$

Now combine like terms:

- $u^2 - u^2 = 0$
- $3u - 3u = 0$
- $-4 + 18 = 14$

So the entire expression simplifies to:

$$
\boxed{14}
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{14}
$$
