

To determine the total surface area of a cube with a volume of 1 cubic foot, we need to follow a clear sequence of steps involving unit conversion and the application of geometric formulas.

---

### Step 1: Understand the Volume of the Cube

The volume $ V $ of a cube is given by the formula:

$$
V = s^3
$$

where $ s $ is the length of one side of the cube in the same unit as the volume. Here, the volume is given as **1 cubic foot**, so:

$$
s^3 = 1 \text{ ft}^3 \Rightarrow s = \sqrt[3]{1} = 1 \text{ ft}
$$

---

### Step 2: Convert the Side Length to Inches

Since the final answer must be in **square inches**, we need to convert the side length from feet to inches. We know:

$$
1 \text{ ft} = 12 \text{ in}
$$

So the side length in inches is:

$$
s = 12 \text{ in}
$$

---

### Step 3: Use the Surface Area Formula

The surface area $ A $ of a cube is given by:

$$
A = 6s^2
$$

Substituting $ s = 12 $ inches:

$$
A = 6 \times (12)^2 = 6 \times 144 = 864 \text{ in}^2
$$

---

### Step 4: Confirm with an Alternative Method

Alternatively, we can convert the volume from **cubic feet to cubic inches** first:

$$
1 \text{ ft}^3 = 12 \times 12 \times 12 = 1728 \text{ in}^3
$$

Now, the volume is 1728 cubic inches, so the side length is:

$$
s = \sqrt[3]{1728} = 12 \text{ in}
$$

Using the same surface area formula:

$$
A = 6 \times (12)^2 = 864 \text{ in}^2
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{864}
$$

The total surface area of the cube is **864 square inches**.
